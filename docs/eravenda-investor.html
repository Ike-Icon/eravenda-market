Eravenda Market — Investor Overview

One platform, two ways for people in Ghana to spend money online.

Eravenda Market connects local sellers with buyers, and connects verified handymen with people who need a job done. Every transaction, product or service, runs through the same platform, the same payments, and the same trust system.

How Eravenda makes money

Sellers and handymen list for free. Eravenda earns a commission only when a sale or a job actually happens, so the platform's income grows directly with how much people buy and book.

A ₵100 sale, step by step

Buyer pays ₵100.00
Eravenda commission (8%) ₵8.00
Payment processing fee ₵2.25
Seller receives ₵89.75

Every sale that completes leaves Eravenda with a share, with no fee charged to sellers who don't sell anything.

No category commission exceeds a 10% platform-wide cap. Rates are lower for thin-margin categories like groceries and higher for categories with more room in the price, in line with what established marketplaces charge in Ghana today, priced to undercut them.

Why buyers and sellers trust the platform

Online buying and hiring both carry risk when money changes hands before anyone has confirmed the product arrived or the job is done. Eravenda is built to remove that risk on both sides.

Verified sellers & pros

Every seller and handyman goes through an identity check before their listings go live.

Payment held until confirmed

For handyman jobs, payment is held by the platform and only released once the customer confirms the work is done.

Cash on delivery, too

Buyers who don't trust paying upfront can choose to pay when the order physically arrives.

Admin oversight on every payout

Every seller and handyman payout is visible to Eravenda's admin team before funds move, catching problems before money leaves the platform.

A brand built to look established from day one

Trust in Ghanaian e-commerce is earned partly through how a platform looks and feels. Eravenda's colors and type were chosen to read as a serious, permanent business, not a side project.
