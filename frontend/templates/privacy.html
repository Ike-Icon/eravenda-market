Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

This is a starter template, not a finished legal document. If you handle real customer data, have this reviewed against Ghana's Data Protection Act (and any other applicable law) before relying on it.

1. What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone), delivery addresses, order history, and — for sellers — store and product information. We don't collect or store your payment card details; those are handled directly by Paystack.

2. How we use it

To process orders, deliver purchases, communicate with you about your account or orders, and to review new stores and product listings before they go live.

3. Who we share it with

Sellers see the delivery details needed to fulfill your order. Paystack processes payment information directly. We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data retention

We keep account and order data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to meet legal and accounting obligations afterward.

5. Your choices

You can update your profile details at any time from your account page. To request deletion of your account, contact us.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us.
