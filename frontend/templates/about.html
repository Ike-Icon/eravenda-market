About Us

Your Marketplace. Reimagined.

EraVenda is where Ghana shops. We bring together the products you need and the professionals you can count on — all in one place, with real reviews and secure payments.

What we do

We run a marketplace. Independent sellers list their products alongside verified professionals who offer their services — and you get everything in one storefront. No more jumping between WhatsApp vendors, Instagram pages, and word-of-mouth recommendations. It's all here, organized, reviewed, and easy to buy from.

What you can find

Products

Electronics, phones, fashion, beauty, home appliances, groceries, health and wellness — from hundreds of verified sellers across Ghana.

Handyman Hub

Repairs, cleaning, installations, tutoring, fitness coaching and more — from reviewed professionals you can book online.

Why people trust us

  • Verified sellers. Every store is reviewed before it goes live. We check who's selling and what they're listing.
  • Real reviews. Ratings come from verified buyers and service customers — not bots, not paid placements.
  • Secure payments. Card, mobile money, or cash on delivery — every method is encrypted and protected.
  • Tracked delivery. Follow your order from purchase to doorstep with real-time updates.

Where we operate

Ghana first. Pricing in cedis (₵), delivery across the country, and payment options that match how people actually shop here — including mobile money. We're building this for our market, our sellers, and our communities.

For sellers & professionals

If you sell products or offer services, EraVenda gives you a storefront, order management, and access to customers who are already looking for what you offer. Open a store or register as a professional — it takes minutes.
