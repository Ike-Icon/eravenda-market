About Eravenda Market

Eravenda Market is a home for independent sellers across Ghana — from a phone shop in Kumasi to a fashion boutique in Accra — to reach buyers without building their own website or payment system from scratch.

We handle the storefront, search, cart, checkout, and payment processing. Sellers focus on what they do best: sourcing good products and getting them delivered.

How it works

Buyers browse products from many independent sellers in one place. Sellers register a store, list products, and get notified the moment an order comes in. Every new store and every new product listing is reviewed before it goes live, so buyers can shop with confidence.

Where we operate

We're focused on Ghana first — delivery, pricing in Ghana cedis (₵), and support for how people actually shop and pay here, including mobile money.
